About

Detail-oriented specialist with proven expertise in compliance and risk management

I'm a detail-oriented AML/OFAC/KYC/Fraud analyst with over 7 years of professional experience across the Financial and Healthcare industries. My expertise spans transaction monitoring, case management, fraud detection, and high-risk PEP screening.

What sets me apart is my integration of advanced technical frameworks—Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF) and Computer-Aided Design (CAD)—into process optimization, elevating investigative workflows and operational controls.

I'm passionate about enhancing organizational value through dynamic compliance strategies, meticulous SAR drafting, and innovative risk mitigation approaches that don't just meet regulatory standards—they exceed them.
